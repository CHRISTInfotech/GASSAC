Who we are

About GASSAC

Global Academia of Scholarly Skill Alliances and Collaboration (GASSAC) is an academic and professional organization established in 2026 with the objective of strengthening education, research, innovation and international collaboration.

The organization provides a platform where educators, researchers, students, professionals and institutions can collaborate, exchange knowledge and contribute toward solving global challenges.

What we seek to achieve

Our Objectives

Eleven commitments guide our programs, partnerships and service to the academic community.

Fostering Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Advance knowledge, promote collaboration and address key global challenges through interdisciplinary dialogue, seminars and conferences organized with host colleges and universities.

Promoting Global Knowledge Sharing

Provide platforms for sharing the latest research, innovation and best practices across the worldwide academic community.

Addressing Global Challenges

Focus academic attention on ecology, environmental concerns, poverty, technological advancement and human rights.

Encouraging Inclusivity and Diversity

Represent and promote diverse perspectives in discussions held through seminars, workshops and conferences.

Enhancing Academic and Professional Development

Create networking and mentorship opportunities that help early-career scholars and researchers build lasting collaborative relationships and achieve long-term growth.

Promoting Education and Capacity Building

Improve academic quality through knowledge sharing, online add-on courses, foreign-language learning, development skills and capacity building for younger learners.

Strengthening Academic Institutions

Promote collaboration among universities, colleges, research institutions and academic organizations. Encourage research projects and case studies that make education engaging, excellent and competitive.

Scientific Literacy and Public Engagement

Improve scientific understanding through community programs, workshops and online courses, while connecting educators and experts through seminars in India and abroad.

Supporting Emerging Scholars

Give early-career researchers and young academicians opportunities to present their work, receive feedback, connect with peers and access resources and mentorship.

Providing Research Funding and Grants

Offer scholarships and fellowships to deserving scholars after proper scrutiny, supporting their education, research projects and international collaboration.

Multilingual Journals and Magazines

Develop publications in English, Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati, Assamese and other relevant languages. Guided by an advisory board, these platforms will exchange ideas, enrich literature, inspire learners and remain responsive to the evolving needs of global society.
