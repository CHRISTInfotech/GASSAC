Eligibility

A Community Built Around Shared Purpose.

The organization may admit scholars, students, teachers, professors, social activists and educators who, in the opinion of the Executive Committee, are interested in advancing GASSAC’s objectives.

  • Resident in any part of India or abroad
  • At least 16 years of age
  • Should not be insane or insolvent
  • Interested in the objectives of the organization
  • Committed to placing the society’s interests above all

Note

Termination of Membership

A person shall cease to be a member of the society in any one of the following events:

  1. If he or she resigns.
  2. If he or she is adjudged insolvent.
  3. If he or she becomes insane.
  4. If he or she is convicted of an offence connected with the formation, promotion, management or conduct of the affairs of the organization, or of any offence involving moral turpitude.
  5. For Annual and Student Members, if the membership fee is not paid within two months of the beginning of the next financial year.
