Welcome to GASSAC

Founded in 2026, GASSAC is a non-profit organization committed to educational excellence, professional development, research collaboration and human welfare.

We connect educators, academicians, researchers, students and institutions across India and the world to create opportunities for learning, innovation and global collaboration.
